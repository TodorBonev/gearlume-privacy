Privacy Policy

Gearlume — a puzzle game for Android by Virevon · com.virevon.gearlume
Effective 23 October 2026

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. I do not run a server for the game.
  • Your progress, stars, settings and daily streak are stored only on your phone. I never receive them.
  • I, the developer, do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect data for that, as described below. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • The game is not directed to children.

1. Who I am

Gearlume is made by Virevon (me). If you have any question about this policy, write to me at quantumesqy@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps a few small files in its private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: which levels you completed and the stars you earned;
  • the level you have started but not finished, and your daily streak;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, theme, language and reduced motion;
  • your hint stock, and whether you bought Remove ads.

These files are never sent to me or to anyone else. Other apps cannot read them. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android’s settings.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Gearlume is free. It is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, using Google’s Mobile Ads SDK. An optional Remove ads in-app purchase is available (see section 4).

  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one to get a hint or to see a solution.
  • Interstitial ads may appear between levels.

Ads are limited to content rated up to T (teen) in AdMob.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use information such as your Android advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location that can be derived from it), device and operating-system details, the app’s name and version, and how you interact with ads and whether they load. Google uses this to choose and show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse, and — depending on your consent and the rules where you live — to personalise ads. Google is responsible for this data. I have no access to it; from AdMob I receive only totals such as the number of ads shown and the revenue.

Your consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows Google’s consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or not shown.
  • Where this form is shown, you can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • On your phone you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Android’s settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).

More about how Google handles this information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy Remove ads inside the game. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. I never see your card details, name or address; Google only tells the game that the purchase went through, which the game remembers on your phone as described above. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. What the game does not do

  • It has no account, log-in or user profile.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or cookies of its own. (The ads described in section 3 are Google’s and may use their own identifiers, cookies or similar technologies.)
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It has no chat or social features and does not show other players’ data.

The game asks Android for these permissions:

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to load ads and to check the connection. Used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): the advertising ID, used for ads as described in section 3.
  • Android’s ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): Android’s Privacy Sandbox ad services, used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK where your device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): the optional Remove ads purchase through Google Play.
  • Keeping the device awake and running a short task (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the background-task library (WorkManager) of Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, to finish short background tasks. The game itself does not run in the background.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): haptic feedback only.

The game does not ask for any other permission, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

If a future version adds anything that changes this — for example analytics — I will update this policy before that version is released.

6. Sharing

I do not collect personal data, so I have none to share or sell. The only other parties involved are Google (ads and payments), as described above, which act under their own privacy policies.

7. Children

Gearlume is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live, for example 16 in some EU countries). I do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the game collects no personal data itself. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact me and I will help.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data that Google collects for ads is kept and can be managed as described in Google’s policies and in your Google account and Android settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom or some US states) you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawing consent. Because I do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access or delete; for data Google collects through the ads, use the choices above, your Google account controls, or contact Google. You can withdraw ad consent at any time as described in section 3. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. You are welcome to contact me with any question.

10. Security and international processing

Your game data sits in the game’s private storage on your phone and is protected by Android. Payments and ads are handled by Google, which processes data in many countries under its own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

When I change this policy I will update the date at the top of this page. The current version is always available at this address.

Играта иска от Android тези разрешения:

  • Задържане на устройството будно и кратка задача (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): добавени от библиотеката за фонови задачи (WorkManager) на Mobile Ads SDK на Google, за да завършва кратки фонови задачи. Самата игра не работи във фонов режим.
  • Вибрация (VIBRATE): само за тактилна обратна връзка.

Играта не иска други разрешения, например за местоположение, контакти, камера, микрофон, снимки или файлове.

Ако бъдеща версия добави нещо, което променя това — например аналитика — ще обновя тази политика, преди тази версия да бъде пусната.
