The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. I do not run a server for the game.

  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect data for that, as described below. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • The game is not directed to children.

1. Who I am

Gearlume is made by Virevon (me). If you have any question about this policy, write to me at quantumesqy@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps a few small files in its private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: which levels you completed and the stars you earned;
  • the level you have started but not finished, and your daily streak;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, theme, language and reduced motion;
  • your hint stock, and whether you bought Remove ads.

These files are never sent to me or to anyone else. Other apps cannot read them. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android’s settings.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Gearlume is free. It is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, using Google’s Mobile Ads SDK. An optional Remove ads in-app purchase is available (see section 5).

  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one to get a hint or to see a solution.
  • Interstitial ads may appear between levels.

Ads are limited to content rated up to PG (parental guidance) in AdMob, matching the game’s PEGI 3 rating.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use information such as your Android advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location that can be derived from it), device and operating-system details, the app’s name and version, and how you interact with ads and whether they load. Google uses this to choose and show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse, and — depending on your consent and the rules where you live — to personalise ads. Google is responsible for this data. I have no access to it; from AdMob I receive only totals such as the number of ads shown and the revenue.

Your consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows Google’s consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or not shown.
  • Where this form is shown, you can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • On your phone you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Android’s settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).

More about how Google handles this information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, Gearlume uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google. It records events about how the game is used:

  • Game events: a level started, won or left; the moves, par, stars and time of a level; hints used; a solution shown; an ad shown; and the daily puzzle played.
  • Automatic events that Firebase records on its own: first open, sessions and screens.

Each event is sent together with an app instance ID (a random identifier of this installation of the game), your device model, operating-system version, app version and approximate country, which is derived from your IP address.

No account, name, email address or advertising ID is collected for analytics.

Your consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics runs only if you consent in Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Outside the EEA and the UK, analytics is on by default.
  • In US states with privacy laws, the Don’t sell or share my data choice in Privacy options applies to ads, not to these game statistics.

Google Analytics keeps event-level data for 2 months, and data linked to the app instance ID for up to 14 months (this period starts again when the game is used again). Aggregated reports are kept longer.

This data is processed by Google. More about how Google handles it: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy Remove ads inside the game. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. I never see your card details, name or address; Google only tells the game that the purchase went through, which the game remembers on your phone as described above. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. What the game does not do

  • It has no account, log-in or user profile.
  • It has no tracking or cookies of its own. (The analytics described in section 4 and the ads described in section 3 are Google’s and may use their own identifiers, cookies or similar technologies.)
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It has no chat or social features and does not show other players’ data.

The game asks Android for these permissions:

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to load ads, send analytics events and check the connection. Used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK and Firebase.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): the advertising ID, used for ads as described in section 3.
  • Android’s ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): Android’s Privacy Sandbox ad services, used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK where your device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): the optional Remove ads purchase through Google Play.
  • Install source from Google Play (com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): lets Google Play tell the analytics library how the game was installed.
  • Keeping the device awake and running a short task (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the background-task library (WorkManager) of Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, to finish short background tasks. The game itself does not run in the background.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): haptic feedback only.

The game does not ask for any other permission, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Sharing

I do not sell your data for money. The only other parties involved are Google (ads, analytics and payments), as described above, which act under their own privacy policies. Google’s ads may use data for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as selling or sharing; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy options.

8. Children

Gearlume is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live, for example 16 in some EU countries). I do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the game itself asks for no name, email or account details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact me and I will help.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data that Google collects for ads and analytics is kept and can be managed as described in Google’s policies and in your Google account and Android settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom or some US states) you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawing consent. Because I keep no account and no details that identify you, there is little for me to access or delete myself; for data Google collects through ads and analytics, use the choices above, your Google account controls, or contact Google. You can withdraw ad and analytics consent at any time as described in sections 3 and 4. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. You are welcome to contact me with any question.

11. Security and international processing

Your game data sits in the game’s private storage on your phone and is protected by Android. Payments, ads and analytics are handled by Google, which processes data in many countries under its own safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy
